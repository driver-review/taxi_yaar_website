Questions

Bangalore to Mysore taxi: FAQs

How far is Mysore from Bangalore by road?

About 140 km by road (Bengaluru–Mysuru Expressway (NH 275)). The drive usually takes 2½–3 hours, depending on traffic and stops.

What is the taxi fare from Bangalore to Mysore?

There's no fixed fare. Post your trip in the TaxiYaar app and drivers on the route send you their price, saying whether toll and parking are included. You compare bids and pick one. You pay the driver directly in cash or by UPI, and TaxiYaar takes no commission or booking fee.

Which car should I book for Bangalore to Mysore?

A sedan is comfortable for up to four people. Pick an SUV for five to seven, or for lots of luggage. You choose the car type when you post your trip.

Can I book a round trip or keep the driver for a few days?

Yes, if the driver agrees. Call them before you accept and agree the return, the waiting and the price. Any extra charges have to be agreed before the trip starts, not during or after.

How do I know the driver is safe?

Every driver's Aadhaar, driving licence and vehicle RC are checked by a person before they go live. You see photos of the actual car and reviews from riders who travelled with them. The trip only starts with your OTP.
