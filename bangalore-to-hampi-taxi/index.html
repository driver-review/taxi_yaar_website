Questions

Bangalore to Hampi taxi: FAQs

How far is Hampi from Bangalore by road?

About 340 km by road (NH 48 via Tumakuru and Chitradurga). The drive usually takes 6–6½ hours, depending on traffic and stops.

What is the taxi fare from Bangalore to Hampi?

There's no fixed fare. Post your trip in the TaxiYaar app and drivers on the route send you their price, saying whether toll and parking are included. You compare bids and pick one. You pay the driver directly in cash or by UPI, and TaxiYaar takes no commission or booking fee.

Which car should I book for Bangalore to Hampi?

A sedan is fine. An SUV for a group or for rough village roads. You choose the car type when you post your trip.

Can I book a round trip or keep the driver for a few days?

Yes, if the driver agrees. Call them before you accept and agree the return, the waiting and the price. Any extra charges have to be agreed before the trip starts, not during or after.

How do I know the driver is safe?

Every driver's Aadhaar, licence and RC are cross-checked before they go live: the face must match the ID, and the number plate in the car photos must match the RC. You see photos of the actual car and reviews from riders who travelled with them. The trip only starts with your OTP.

Will a different car turn up than the one I booked?

No. Every bid shows the car model and photos of that exact car, so you know which car is coming before you book. And the trip starts only when you share your OTP with the driver.
