Questions

Bangalore airport taxi: FAQs

How much is a taxi to Bangalore airport?

There's no fixed fare: drivers send you their price and you choose. As a guide, riders on TaxiYaar booked sedans at ₹1,000–1,200, mostly with toll included, for 45–50 km airport trips from south-east Bengaluru in September and October 2026. Your trip gets its own bids, and the price depends on where you start.

Is there surge pricing for early-morning or late-night flights?

No. TaxiYaar has no surge pricing and no app-set price at all. Drivers quote their own price for your trip, and TaxiYaar adds nothing on top: no booking fee and no commission.

Are toll and airport parking included?

Every bid says whether toll and parking are included, so you can compare like with like before you accept.

Can I book the night before an early flight?

Yes. Post the trip for any date and time. Book ahead for very early pickups, when fewer drivers are awake to bid.

How do I know who is driving me?

You choose the driver yourself. Before you accept, you see photos of the actual car, the driver's ratings in 8 categories and reviews from riders who travelled with them. Every driver's Aadhaar, licence and RC are cross-checked before they go live: the face must match the ID, and the number plate in the car photos must match the RC.
