Delete My Data

You can ask us to permanently delete your TaxiYaar account and everything linked to it. We do it within 30 days and confirm on WhatsApp.

Quickest — from the app

  1. Open the TaxiYaar or TaxiYaar Driver app
  2. Open the side menu and tap Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account — WhatsApp opens with your request ready. Press send.

What gets deleted

  • Your phone number and name
  • All your trip requests and bid history
  • Reviews you have submitted
  • Driver documents and car photos (driver accounts)
  • Your entire account
