How a trip works

From request to drop-off, you're choosing a person.

  1. Now

    Post your trip

    Route, date, time and passengers. Your request is live for an hour and drivers on your route are notified straight away.

  2. Minutes

    Drivers bid

    Each bid carries a price, a note, and whether toll and parking are included — no surprises later.

  3. Your pace

    Pick on trust

    Every bid opens the full profile: 8 category ratings, 4 car photos, reviews from real trips.

  4. One call

    Call and accept

    Talk to the driver directly, then accept their bid. Your trip is confirmed.

  5. Pickup

    Share your OTP

    The driver starts the ride with your trip code — only you have it.

    4827
  6. Drop-off

    Pay and review

    Pay the driver directly. Rate them across all 8 categories for the next rider.

The driver profile

A star average tells you nothing.

You'll spend hours in this car. So every driver is rated on eight things that matter on a long road — and you see all of them before you call.
