Questions

Intercity taxis from Bangalore, answered.

Can I book an intercity taxi from Bangalore on TaxiYaar?

Yes. Any trip that starts in Bengaluru, whether that's Mysuru, Coorg, Ooty, Chennai, Tirupati, Hyderabad or Goa. Post your trip in the app and verified drivers on your route bid for it.

How much does an outstation cab from Bangalore cost?

There's no fixed fare. Drivers set their own price in their bid and say whether toll and parking are included. You compare bids, pick one and pay the driver directly in cash or by UPI. TaxiYaar takes no commission or booking fee.

How are TaxiYaar drivers verified?

Every driver's Aadhaar, licence and RC are cross-checked before they go live: the name and date of birth must match, the face must match the ID, and the number plate in the car photos must match the RC. You also see photos of the actual car and reviews from riders who travelled with them.

Will a different car turn up than the one I booked?

No. Every bid shows the car model and photos of that exact car, so you know which car is coming before you book. And the trip starts only when you share your OTP with the driver.
