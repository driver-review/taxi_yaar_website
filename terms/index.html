3 How a Booking Works

  • Request: A Rider posts a trip request with pickup and drop locations, travel date and time, number of passengers, luggage, and preferred car type. A request stays open for a limited time and then expires.
  • Bids: Drivers who see the request may offer a price ("bid") and state whether tolls and parking are included.
  • Choosing: The Rider can view Driver profiles, ratings, and vehicle photos, and may call a Driver before deciding.
  • Confirmation: When the Rider accepts a bid, the trip is confirmed at that price and both sides are notified.
  • Starting the trip: The Rider receives a trip OTP. The Driver must enter it to start the trip. Riders should share the OTP only once they are with the Driver and in the correct vehicle.
  • Completion: The Driver marks the trip complete at the end, and the Rider may then rate the Driver.

4 Fares and Payment

  • The fare is the price in the bid the Rider accepted. Tolls and parking are included only if the bid says so; otherwise they are paid by the Rider as actually incurred.
  • Payment is made directly between the Rider and the Driver (for example, cash or UPI). TaxiYaar does not collect, hold, or process trip payments.
  • TaxiYaar currently charges neither Riders nor Drivers any fee or commission. If we introduce a fee in future, we will tell you in advance and you can choose whether to continue.
  • Drivers must not demand more than the agreed fare. Any other charges (such as waiting time, extra stops, or a change of route) must be agreed by both sides before the trip, not during or after it.
  • Any payment disagreement is between the Rider and the Driver. We may help where we can, but we are not responsible for payments we never handled.

5 Cancellations and No-Shows

  • We keep a record of cancellations and no-shows. Frequent cancellations or no-shows may affect a Driver's visibility to Riders, or lead to restrictions on either account.
